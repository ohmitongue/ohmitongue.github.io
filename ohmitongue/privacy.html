Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Ohmitongue (“the app”) is an educational game about electricity and electronics. This policy explains what information the app uses. We keep it short because the app collects very little.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, saved ideas, unlocked bonus content and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not run a server that receives this data. If you clear the app’s data or uninstall the app, it is deleted.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob, a Google service, may collect and use your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions you will be asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in the app under About and settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings. See Google’s explanation at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Google Play. We only learn whether the purchase exists on your Google account so we can turn ads off. We do not see your payment details.

Fonts

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives your IP address in the request, as with any web request.

Children

The app is meant for a general audience of age 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone ourselves. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob, Google Play and Google Fonts), which handle data under their own policies.

Your choices

You can change ad consent in the app, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, delete your progress in the app (About and settings, Reset progress) or by clearing the app data, and remove ads with the one-time purchase.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: ohmitongue.app@gmail.com
